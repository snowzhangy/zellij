Privacy Policy

Zelmux is designed as a local-first client for user-operated Zellij web servers. The app developer does not collect, sell, or share personal data from the app.

Data the app stores on your device

Zelmux stores app settings, server profiles, session names, display preferences, and prompt history locally on your device. Authentication tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain.

Data sent to your server

When you connect, Zelmux sends your configured server URL, authentication token, terminal input, resize events, and optional image uploads to the Zellij server you choose. This traffic goes directly between your device and your server.

Data not collected by us

  • No analytics are collected.
  • No advertising identifiers are collected.
  • No account or contact information is collected by the app developer.
  • No terminal content is sent to the app developer.

Notifications

Zelmux may request sound-only notification permission so terminal bell events can play a short ding. Notification content does not include terminal text.

Security

Zelmux supports certificate fingerprint trust for user-operated servers. You are responsible for protecting your server URL, authentication token, and server machine.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the contact method listed in the App Store listing.
